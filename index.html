01

2026 · In progress · Solo project

Sola — a 2D room planner

A room planner that checks whether a layout actually works, not just whether the furniture fits. It started at 3am while planning a shared dorm room with my sister — trying to figure out beds and desks that gave us both privacy, good light, and room to walk. Sola flags blocked walkways, drawer and chair clearance, and door-swing space as you place furniture, and will eventually estimate how sunlight moves through the room by location, orientation, and time of year.
